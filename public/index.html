The family caregiver ends up holding everything.

It gets passed along by text, phone call and memory, and things fall through.

  • The aide misses changes. A new dose or instruction from the doctor doesn't reach the person giving the care.
  • The doctor misses the weeks between visits. What the caregivers saw at home never makes it to the appointment.
  • Follow-ups have no owner. After a visit or discharge, nobody is clearly responsible for the refill, the recheck or the next booking.
  • The record is scattered. It lives in a paper folder, a group text and a calendar on the fridge.

Three roles, one profile.

Everyone signs in with their own account and sees the same information from their own phone or computer. Every entry shows who made it and when.

Family caregiver

Coordinates the care

Creates the profile, invites the care team, builds the weekly schedule and keeps track of follow-ups.

Assisting caregiver

Starts each visit caught up

Home aides, nurses and therapists check the schedule and the last note, then log each visit.

Physician

Sees between visits quickly

Doctors and their office staff record diagnoses and notes, and upload charts and written prescriptions.

Four tabs that cover the week.

Schedule

A repeating weekly caregiver schedule, plus follow-up appointments and prescription pick-ups with who's responsible and when. Overdue items are flagged.

Medical History

Diagnoses marked active or resolved, medications, allergies, mobility, daily needs, the primary physician and an emergency contact.

Doctor's notes

Dated notes from each doctor, linked to a diagnosis, plus a place to keep medical charts and written prescriptions.

Logs

A short note after every visit, stamped with who wrote it and when, so the next person starts caught up.

Privacy comes before the pilot.

Care Loop will hold health information about a real person, so these protections have to be in place before any real patient information goes in:

  • Each person sees only the profiles they own or were invited to.
  • What you can change depends on your role on that profile.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • A record of who viewed and changed what.
  • The owner can export a profile and delete it completely.
  • No health data is sent to analytics tools.
  • Session timeouts, strong passwords and two-step sign-in.
  • A privacy policy and terms published before launch.

Care Loop is in early development and is not yet ready for real patient information. Please don't enter real health details yet.
